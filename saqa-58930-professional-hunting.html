Who this qualification is for

  • New entrants who want a nationally registered qualification in professional hunting, trained in the field in Limpopo.
  • Practising professional hunters who already hold a PH licence and want it recognised as a SAQA-registered qualification. See how to convert a PH licence to SAQA credits at Limpopo Wildlife Eco College.
  • Hunting outfitters and conservation employers who need staff with a verifiable, quality-assured NQF Level 4 qualification.

We are also accredited for the Professional Hunter Dangerous Game skills programme (HUNT/PROFHUNTDANG/4/0060, NQF Level 4).

How you are assessed

Learners build a Portfolio of Evidence (PoE) from their field work. Limpopo Wildlife Eco College students record field days, trail hours and dangerous-game encounters in the college's Field Logbook. Each entry is pinned to GPS and signed off by a mentor, and the logbook prints as a ready-to-submit Portfolio of Evidence. A registered assessor assesses the portfolio against the qualification, and it is then moderated before results are submitted for certification.

Frequently asked

Is Limpopo Wildlife Eco College accredited for SAQA 58930?

Yes. Africa Expectation Safari's t/a Limpopo Wildlife Eco College is an accredited provider for SAQA Qualification 58930. We are accredited for it by CATHSSETA, the qualification's quality assurance functionary (CATHSSETA Accreditation Number 613/P/00392/2022). We are also a QCTO-accredited Skills Development Provider (09-QCTO/SDP261022055054). Our school director holds a LEDET Nature Conservation permit to act as Professional Hunting School Director.

Can I still enrol for SAQA Qualification 58930?

Yes. SAQA gives 30 June 2028 as the last date for enrolment and 30 June 2031 as the last date for achievement. Enrol before 30 June 2028 to make sure you can complete the qualification in time.

Why does the SAQA database show "Africa Expectation Safari's"?

Africa Expectation Safari's is our registered legal name, and accreditation is issued to the legal entity. Limpopo Wildlife Eco College is our trading name. They are the same provider.

I already hunt professionally. Do I have to do the full course?

Not necessarily. Through RPL, your licence, hunting registers and field experience count as evidence. You only train in the areas where your evidence doesn't yet meet the standard.
