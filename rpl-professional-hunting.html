How to convert a PH licence to SAQA credits at Limpopo Wildlife Eco College

A professional hunter (PH) licence shows you are allowed to guide hunts. It is not a SAQA-registered qualification, and it is not converted automatically. Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) is the route that turns your licence and years in the field into credits on the National Qualifications Framework. Your existing evidence is assessed against the Further Education and Training Certificate: Professional Hunting (SAQA ID 58930, NQF Level 4), and you are credited for every outcome you can prove.

  1. Enquire and pre-screen. Contact the college. An RPL advisor checks your licence, experience and documents against SAQA Qualification 58930 and tells you honestly whether RPL suits you.
  2. Gather your evidence. Collect what you already have (see the checklist below).
  3. Compile your RPL portfolio. With the college's guidance, you arrange your evidence against the qualification outcomes. Where you need fresh records, the college's Field Logbook captures GPS-pinned, mentor-signed field entries and prints them as a Portfolio of Evidence.
  4. Assessment. A registered assessor reviews your portfolio and may observe you in the field or interview you to confirm competence.
  5. Close any gaps. Where evidence falls short, you complete targeted training for those areas only.
  6. Moderation and certification. Your results are moderated and submitted to the quality assurance body for certification.

RPL portfolio evidence checklist

  • Certified copy of your ID or passport
  • Your professional hunter licence (current and previous)
  • Hunting registers or client hunt records
  • Firearm competency certificates
  • First aid certificate
  • Letters of reference from outfitters, landowners or employers
  • Any previous certificates or unit standard results
  • Field records: dates, places, species and the role you played, ideally GPS-pinned and signed by a mentor

The assessor confirms the final evidence requirements for your portfolio during pre-screening. Your evidence is assessed against the professional hunting unit standards in our accredited scope (SAQA IDs 246412 to 246422), for example "Hunt wild animals with a client" (246413) and "Pursue a dangerous game quarry" (246417).

Frequently asked

Can I convert my PH licence to SAQA credits?

Not automatically. Through RPL at Limpopo Wildlife Eco College, your licence, hunting registers and field experience become evidence. That evidence is assessed against SAQA Qualification 58930, and credits are awarded for the outcomes you can prove.

Who is an accredited provider for RPL on SAQA Qualification 58930?

Africa Expectation Safari's t/a Limpopo Wildlife Eco College is accredited by CATHSSETA, the qualification's quality assurance functionary, as a provider for SAQA Qualification 58930. CATHSSETA Accreditation Number: 613/P/00392/2022.

What if my evidence does not cover the whole qualification?

You receive credit for what you can prove and complete targeted training only for the remaining outcomes, instead of repeating the full course.

Can I still enrol for SAQA Qualification 58930?

Yes. SAQA gives 30 June 2028 as the last date for enrolment and 30 June 2031 as the last date for achievement, so start your RPL application well before June 2028.
