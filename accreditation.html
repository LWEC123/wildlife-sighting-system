CATHSSETA provider accreditation

CATHSSETA has accredited Africa Expectation Safari's t/a Limpopo Wildlife Eco College as a provider under ETQA Regulation R1127 of the SAQA Act (Act 58 of 1995). The accreditation runs from 30 March 2022 to 29 March 2027, or for the lifespan of the unit standards, subject to prevailing legislation. The scope below is taken from CATHSSETA's confirmation letter of 25 April 2025: 3 full qualifications, 3 skills programmes and 87 unit standards.

QCTO Skills Development Provider accreditation

The Quality Council for Trades and Occupations (QCTO) has accredited Africa Expectation Safaris t/a Limpopo Wildlife Eco College as a Skills Development Provider (SDP) under the Skills Development Act, 1998 (Act No. 97 of 1998), Chapter 6C, after a site inspection. The accreditation runs for 5 years, from 26 October 2022 to 25 October 2027.
