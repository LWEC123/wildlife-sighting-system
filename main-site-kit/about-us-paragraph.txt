Africa Expectation Safari's t/a Limpopo Wildlife Eco College. "Africa Expectation Safari's" is our registered legal name. It is the name that appears in the SAQA database and on our accreditation records. "Limpopo Wildlife Eco College" is the name we trade and teach under. We are accredited by CATHSSETA (Accreditation Number: 613/P/00392/2022, valid 30 March 2022 to 29 March 2027), by the QCTO (Accreditation Number: 09-QCTO/SDP261022055054) and our school director holds a LEDET Nature Conservation permit to act as Professional Hunting School Director (valid 20 August 2024 to 20 August 2027). We are an accredited provider for SAQA Qualification 58930, the Further Education and Training Certificate: Professional Hunting (NQF Level 4). We are also accredited for the FETC: Tourist Guiding (SAQA 71549), the National Certificate: Accommodation Services (SAQA 14110), and the Professional Hunter Dangerous Game and Dangerous Game Site Guide skills programmes.
