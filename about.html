Who we are

Limpopo Wildlife Eco College is an accredited provider of professional hunting, conservation and field skills training in Limpopo, South Africa. Our training happens in the bush. Students build a Portfolio of Evidence from real field days, trail hours and dangerous-game encounters, recorded in the college's own Field Logbook.

We are an accredited provider for SAQA Qualification 58930, the Further Education and Training Certificate: Professional Hunting. Practising professional hunters can also gain this qualification through Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL), with their existing licence and hunting record counting as evidence.

Our school director

Our school director, Elfrieda Wessels, holds a permit from LEDET (Limpopo Department of Economic Development, Environment and Tourism, Directorate: Wildlife Trade and Regulation) to act as Professional Hunting School Director. It was issued under the Limpopo Environmental Management Act, 2003 (Act No. 7 of 2003) and is valid from 20 August 2024 to 20 August 2027.

A professional hunting school director is the head of a professional hunting school approved by the provincial nature conservation authority. The director oversees the theory and practical training of aspiring professional hunters. The director also signs the course completion certificates that learners need when they apply for a provincial Professional Hunter permit.

Unit standards

The scope also covers the unit standards that make up these qualifications. They include the professional hunting unit standards (SAQA IDs 246412 to 246422), "Track animals and identify spoor using easily recognisable spoor" (8460), "Conduct a guided nature experience in a limited geographical area" (8440), "Create a guided experience for customers" (8478) and "Provide First Aid" (7854). See the full accreditation scope for all 87 unit standards.

SAQA lists 30 June 2028 as the last date for enrolment and 30 June 2031 as the last date for achievement for both SAQA 58930 and SAQA 71549.
