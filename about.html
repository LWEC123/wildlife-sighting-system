Who we are

Limpopo Wildlife Eco College is an accredited provider of professional hunting, conservation and field skills training in Limpopo, South Africa. Our training happens in the bush. Students build a Portfolio of Evidence from real field days, trail hours and dangerous-game encounters, recorded in the college's own Field Logbook.

We are an accredited provider for SAQA Qualification 58930, the Further Education and Training Certificate: Professional Hunting. Practising professional hunters can also gain this qualification through Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL), with their existing licence and hunting record counting as evidence.

CATHSSETA-accredited programmes

CATHSSETA's accreditation letter to Africa Expectation Safari's (provider accreditation number 613/P/00392/2022) lists the following programmes:

Full qualifications

  • Further Education and Training Certificate: Professional Hunting (SAQA ID 58930, NQF Level 4), including RPL for practising professional hunters
  • Further Education and Training Certificate: Tourist Guiding (SAQA ID 71549, NQF Level 4)
  • National Certificate: Accommodation Services (SAQA ID 14110, NQF Level 2)

Skills programmes

  • Professional Hunter Dangerous Game (HUNT/PROFHUNTDANG/4/0060, NQF Level 4)
  • Dangerous Game Site Guide L4 (TG/DANGAMSITEGD/4/0044, NQF Level 4)
  • Dangerous Game Site Guide L2 (TG/DANGAMSITEGD/2/0043, NQF Level 2)

Unit standards

The scope also covers the unit standards that make up these qualifications. They include the professional hunting unit standards (SAQA IDs 246412 to 246422), "Track animals and identify spoor using easily recognisable spoor" (8460), "Conduct a guided nature experience in a limited geographical area" (8440), "Create a guided experience for customers" (8478) and "Provide First Aid" (7854).

Other courses

  • Tracker Skills Programme
  • 12 Day Professional Hunting Course
