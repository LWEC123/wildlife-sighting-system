🤖 AI help with marking

To get your marks back faster, the college can use an AI tool (Claude, made by Anthropic) to suggest marks for the tests and answer sheets you hand in. Your assessor always checks every mark and makes the final decision: the AI never decides if you are competent.

If you say yes, your answers and uploaded answer sheets (with your name on them) are sent securely to Anthropic, which may process them outside South Africa. Anthropic does not use them to train its AI and keeps them only for a limited time. The college uses this only for marking your work (POPIA, sections 18 and 72). You can change your mind at any time on this page. If you say no, your assessor marks everything by hand, and nothing else changes for you.

📊 Student monitor

"Online now" means active on the course in the last 2 minutes. Time is only counted while a student is actually using the page (it stops after 10 idle minutes).

✍️ Tests waiting to be marked

Marking a test competent opens the next module for that student. Not yet competent lets them try again. Mark against your assessor memo. When the AI has pre-marked a test (🤖), check its suggested marks and comments, change what you disagree with, then confirm with your own decision; the AI never decides. Memos you give the AI go into a locked folder that no browser can open. Every mark is kept, with the student's answers, for their PoE pack.

🤖 Memos for AI pre-marking (answer sheets)

When a student hands in a test, the AI reads their work and the memo, and suggests marks, comments and Competent or Not yet competent. You get an email, then you check it here and confirm. Nothing counts until you click Competent or Not yet competent.

Online tests already use the memo lines you imported with the test. Answer sheets (Word, PDF, photos) need the assessor memo: choose the memo files from 05 Answers › Assessor CONF (e.g. M01_Culture_and_Heritage_Memo.docx). They go into a locked folder: no browser can open them again, not even yours; only the marking server reads them. If a module has no memo here, the AI uses the online-test memo for that module when there is one.

🔑 Give students access

Type each student's name on its own line and press Create codes. Give each student their own code (or print the slips). A student creates their account with their code, or enters it after signing in with their Logbook account. Each code works once, for one student.

One code opens the whole qualification (FETC: Tourist Guiding, SAQA 71549). A student never needs a new code for the next unit standard: every unit standard you publish in Course setup appears for them automatically. To stop a student, or one unit standard for a student, open them in the Student monitor.

🛠️ Course setup

Give each module a title and add each file. Students only ever receive the links of modules they've unlocked. Never paste a link to an assessor memo here.

Protected files: anything uploaded here can only be opened by signed-in students of this qualification, inside the portal. Guides, presentations and tests must be PDF so students can view them without downloading (they see their name and email across every page). Videos must be .mp4 so watching is tracked. The learner answer sheet may be Word: students download it to fill in. Pasted links to other websites (OneDrive, YouTube) are not protected.
Fastest way: click 📂 Upload the whole course folder and choose the unit standard's folder (e.g. US 335815 - Conduct a guided biome experience) on your computer. The portal matches every guide, presentation, video, test and learner answer sheet to its module by the M01, M02… file names, uses the PDF versions in 06 Portal PDFs where they exist, uploads them, and saves the course. Anything in an Assessor, CONF or memo folder, and the Facilitator and Assessor Guide, is skipped automatically.
