Import from your Field Notebook

Sightings you recorded in the Wildlife Sighting System on this phone can be pulled straight into an encounter entry — species, date, time, GPS pin and notes come across, no retyping. Pick one, check everything, choose the dangerous-game category if it applies, and save. Your mentor still reviews and signs as usual.

What are you logging?

📍 Log it where it happens — at the sighting, not later from camp. The GPS pin stamps where your phone is the moment you tap the button, and that is exactly what makes your record credible to your assessor. No signal needed: entries and pins are kept on your phone and upload themselves the moment you're back in range.

Portfolio of Evidence pack

Builds your complete PoE in seconds: a cover page with your details and declaration, a summary of totals, and every entry in chronological order with its recorded mentor signatures. No binder needed: choose “Save as PDF” in the print screen — the electronic PoE (PDF with recorded mentor sign-offs) is accepted by our assessor. Generate it fresh whenever it's needed; it is always complete and up to date.

📄 Data export

Your logbook is stored safely in your college account and synced to every device you sign in on — no manual backups needed any more.

College staff

Everyone holding mentor keys. Full mentors can also correct records (unlock signatures, delete entries); signing mentors review and sign. Staff appear here after their first sign-in since being appointed.
